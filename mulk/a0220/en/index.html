Area report · Elliniko · Athens Riviera

Location and transport

Kato Elliniko lies on the Athens Riviera, right next to the Ellinikon regeneration project rising on the former airport site, about 10 – 12 km from central Athens. The beaches are about 3 minutes away and central Glyfada about 5 minutes by car.

There is direct access to Vouliagmenis Avenue; metro Line 2 is nearby and the airport is about 25 – 30 minutes away. Ellinikon's value effect is spreading to Glyfada, Voula and Alimos, and Kato Elliniko sits right next to the regeneration area. Investment here therefore rests on long-term capital growth rather than current rental yield.

Avla's assessment

The project's strength is its location: adjacent to Ellinikon, about 3 minutes from the beach. Fully furnished handover, together with the pool, roof garden and rooftop lounge, speeds up letting.

The price ladder is narrow: 1-bed units range from €300,000 to €319,000, € / m² net 7,334 – 7,589. Within this band the choice is driven more by floor and outdoor space than by price; the ground floor offers garden or courtyard access and more outdoor space. The 1-bed units are small (40.19 – 42.97 m²). G5, the only available 2-bed, has the project's lowest price per square metre at € / m² net 5,982 — about 20% below the 1-bed average.

The investment profile is long-term: capital growth outweighs current rental yield. Indicative rent and yield calculations are in the feasibility study; the figures are not commitments.

The Framework First

Why does the investment threshold differ from one buyer to the next?

Because there is no single figure. The law defines three tiers , and which tier a property falls under is determined by its location and legal status .

€800,000
Upper tier
Applies in areas under high demand. A single property of at least 120 m² is required.
€400,000
Other regions
The same single-property and minimum 120 m² conditions apply.
€250,000
Not location-dependent
Only in two cases: (a) the change of use of a non-residential building to residential, (b) a restored listed building.

What this means for your file: In Attica the threshold for a standard new build is €800,000. Which threshold the properties in this file fall under is determined by the property's location and legal status ; this is confirmed in writing, property by property , through an independent Greek lawyer's review. Nowhere have we written "this apartment qualifies for the GV"; we obtain the confirmation in writing.

Your money goes not to the seller but to a dual-signature account .

The sale price is not paid into an account the seller can use freely. We go through the figures with documents at the meeting; but so that you set your expectations correctly from the start, here is the framework:

800,000 €
The standard investment threshold across the whole Attica region.
250,000 €

5 years

Announcement · September 2026

Transfer tax 15%

From 1 July 2027

For non-EU/EEA nationals, on residential purchases by individuals the transfer tax rises from 3.09% to 15.45% . Purchases by companies, EU/EEA citizens, long-term residents and people of Greek origin are excluded. The Prime Minister announced it on 6 September and the Ministry of Finance gave details on 8 September — no bill has yet been submitted to Parliament.

 In consultation · June 2026

Portfolio Golden Visa

Subject to long-term letting

The 2026–2035 National Housing Strategy proposes a separate Golden Visa category in which several apartments are bought as a single portfolio and all are let long-term . The threshold, lease term and conditions have not yet been written; the market reading is that it will be an option for the 400,000 / 800,000 € tiers, where the single-property rule applies — this is a proposal, not law.

The €250,000 route

Conversion category

The €250,000 tier is in force today and applies only to properties converted to residential use through a change of use . The sector is asking for this category to be exempted from the new transfer tax — the reasoning: these investments do not take homes off the market, they create new stock. No decision yet.
